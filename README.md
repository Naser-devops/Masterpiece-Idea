🎓 Masterpiece Project — 3 Ideas

Three candidate ideas for my university masterpiece project, each a web platform with a clear business model and AI-powered features.

🚗 1. Car Rental Platform

A platform where car rental companies list their vehicles and customers browse, compare, and book online — no in-person visit needed.

Target audience: rental companies & customers/tourists needing a short-term car
Business model: commission per booking + optional premium listing subscription
AI features: smart car recommendations, dynamic pricing, automatic photo-based condition checks
🏋️ 2. Sports Club Management System

An all-in-one system for gyms/sports clubs to manage member subscriptions, attendance, class bookings, and progress — with automatic alerts when a member's subscription is about to expire.

Target audience: club owners, trainers, and members
Business model: monthly B2B SaaS subscription paid by the club
AI features: churn prediction, personalized workout/nutrition suggestions
🎉 3. Events Planning Platform

A marketplace where people planning an event (wedding, graduation, conference) can browse, compare, and book venues, catering, photography, and decor providers from one place.

Target audience: individuals planning events & event-service providers
Business model: commission per booking + featured-placement subscription for providers
AI features: AI assistant that builds a full event plan by budget/guest count/style, review-quality-based provider ranking
